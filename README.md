One image that sums up the world's news of Tuesday, 24 November 2026: a
newspaper front page — "The World in One Day" — with 23 dated stories across
8 regions. The hero is the U.S. jury verdict on Huawei (guilty of trade
secrets and bank fraud, acquitted of racketeering); six illustrated stories
follow (four tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz, Ukraine's ship
strikes in the Gulf of Finland and off Libya, the Riyadh PIF tower hit,
the ICJ's Myanmar genocide ruling, Senegal's Africa Cup title restored,
Sanofi's $41B Insmed deal), plus a twelve-item "more today" list and a
footer strip. The image is drawn from a hand-written HTML page
(`build/page.html`, rendered to `entry.png` with headless Chromium); the
program just copies it to `/output/entry.png` after checking it is a whole
PNG inside the limits.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
